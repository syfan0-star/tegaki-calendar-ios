• In addition to Japanese, the app can now be shown in English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. It switches to match your device language (on devices set to other languages, it is shown in English).
• To use a different language for this app only, choose it in the Settings app > Apps > Tegaki > Language (on iOS and iPadOS 17: Settings > Tegaki > Preferred Language). If the item is not there, it appears after you add the language you want to use in Settings > General > Language & Region (when you add it, if you are asked which language to use for the display of your device, choose to keep your current language).
• The app can now show the public holidays of countries and regions around the world. In the app, go to Settings > Holidays > “Holidays to Show” to choose a country (and also a region, such as a US state) or “None”. In countries with states or regions, choosing a state or region adds the holidays of that region. Japanese holidays work as before. If you have been using the app, it keeps showing Japanese holidays.
• Weeks can now start on Monday or Sunday (Settings > Week Starts On). If you have been using the app, it stays on Monday. If you are new to the app, it follows your device region. Handwriting on week pages is saved separately for weeks that start on Monday and weeks that start on Sunday. When you switch, the handwriting for the other week start stays hidden until you switch back, but it is not deleted. Handwriting on month pages follows its date. Week handwriting written with a Sunday start cannot be seen on devices with an older version of the app (it has not been deleted).
• Holiday, language and week start settings are saved on each device. When you change the holidays or the language, your handwriting stays in the same place.

The app now follows your device language. The name on the Home Screen is now “Tegaki”.
