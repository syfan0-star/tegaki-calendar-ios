No sign-in, no account creation and no demo account are needed.

What is new in 1.3
1. Week and day pages are joined side by side with the pages before and after them: swiping sideways brings in the neighboring week or day, and a stroke can cross the border. Such a stroke is saved whole on the page holding its center, in the same file and iCloud record format; older versions show it on that page only. Month and year pages stay one page at a time.
2. A new Settings section, Pages, has one switch for this (on by default, per device). Off gives the week and day pages of 1.2.3.
3. A small card explains the swipe for 8 seconds when a joined page first opens (once per device and page type).
4. iCloud sync fixes: handwriting erased with Erase All (here or on another device) or Erase and Start can no longer come back through the open page, Undo or a page fetch and be sent to iCloud again.
Unchanged: data (App Privacy stays "Data Not Collected"), handwriting storage, purchases, the age rating and the age range check of 1.2.2.

The app follows the device language (10 languages since 1.2; any other language shows English). Per-app language: Settings > Apps > Tegaki > Language.

To test: add 2 or 3 events (one all-day, one timed) in the Calendar app, then open the app. The events appear on the month, week, day and year pages, and you can write on them with Apple Pencil, or with a finger after turning on Draw with Finger (in the toolbar or in Settings > Input). Handwriting also works with no events and without iCloud. For 1.3: swipe a week page sideways (one finger on iPad, two on iPhone), write a stroke across the border between two weeks (Saturday to Sunday if weeks start on Sunday, else Sunday to Monday) and try Settings > Pages.

Subscriptions and prices are unchanged (com.syfan0.tegakicalendar.monthly and .yearly, auto-renewable, 14-day free trial). The purchase screen appears once after the first-time guide, and it also opens from the View Only badge in the header and from Settings > Purchase. Restore: Settings > Purchase > Restore Purchases. Links to the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and Apple's standard EULA are on the purchase screen and in Settings > About. The Japanese Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are the governing text; the English pages are translations for convenience and say so.

In TestFlight and App Review builds (sandbox receipt) only, touching and holding Settings > About > Version opens a log sheet titled 「記録」 (in Japanese in every language) that the user can copy and send to the developer; nothing is sent automatically. It holds only the app and OS versions, device model, tool changes, page types, stroke counts, touch types and elapsed times (no handwriting content, dates or personal information), and it never appears in App Store builds.

No server: calendar data is used with EventKit on the device only, and handwriting stays on the device and in the user's own iCloud private database, which the developer cannot access. No analytics, ads or third-party SDKs.
