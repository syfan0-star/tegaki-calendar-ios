No sign-in, no account creation and no demo account are needed.

What is new in 1.2
1. Languages: the app is now localized into 10 languages (Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese). It follows the device language; any other device language shows English. iOS adds the per-app language item: Settings > Apps > Tegaki > Language (Settings > Tegaki > Preferred Language on iOS 17). On non-Japanese devices the Home Screen name is "Tegaki".
2. Holidays: the app shows the public holidays of many countries and regions. The default is the device region; users updating from 1.1 keep Japan. To try it: gear button (Settings) > Holidays > Holidays to Show, then choose a country, then a state or region if it has them (for example United States > California), or None. The data is generated from the open-source python-holidays library (MIT License; full text in Settings > About > Open Source Licenses) and is bundled in the app. No network is used for holidays, so App Privacy stays "Data Not Collected".
By design, weeks start on Monday and times on the pages use the 24-hour clock in every language (the handwriting is tied to fixed positions on the pages).

To test: add 2 or 3 events (one all-day, one timed) in the Calendar app, then open the app. The events appear on the month, week, day and year pages, and you can write on them with Apple Pencil, or with a finger after turning on Draw with Finger (in the toolbar or in Settings > Input). Handwriting also works with no events and without iCloud.

Subscriptions are unchanged (com.syfan0.tegakicalendar.monthly and .yearly, auto-renewable, 14-day free trial; the same products and prices as 1.1). The purchase screen appears once after the first-time guide, and it also opens from the View Only badge in the header and from Settings > Purchase. Restore: Settings > Purchase > Restore Purchases. Links to the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and Apple's standard EULA are on the purchase screen and in Settings > About. The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages are in Japanese (the governing text).

In TestFlight and App Review builds (sandbox receipt) only, touching and holding Settings > About > Version opens a log sheet titled 「記録」 (in Japanese in every language) that the user can copy and send to the developer; it holds only tool changes, page types, stroke counts, touch types and elapsed times, with no handwriting content, no dates and no personal information, and it never appears in App Store builds.

No server: calendar data is used with EventKit on the device only, and handwriting stays on the device and in the user's own iCloud private database, which the developer cannot access. No analytics, ads or third-party SDKs.
